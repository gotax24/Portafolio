## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, engineering leads, and prospective clients evaluating Ernesto Bracho R. (`gotax.dev` / `gotax24`) as an IT Analyst and Full Stack Web Developer based in Maracaibo, Zulia, Venezuela.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio website at `gotax.dev` showcasing Ernesto Bracho's complete career trajectory (from customer service, retail inventory, and technical installations to IT Analyst & Full Stack Developer at CEVAZ) alongside his full catalog of production systems, private enterprise work, in-development projects, and his early learning archive from 2024 to the present. Success means a visitor immediately understands his practical business background, engineering evolution, and real-world systems, and can reach him directly via LinkedIn, Instagram, Email, or GitHub.

## Positioning

Unlike generic developer portfolios that hide non-tech jobs or early beginner repositories, `gotax.dev` presents an honest, chronological trajectory: how years of customer-facing operations, physical inventory control (Farmatodo, EPA, ESSALUD), CCTV/hardware installation (Indsol), and patient operations (CDD Radioterapia) directly informed the architecture of real institutional systems at CEVAZ (`cevaz-rm`, `ICEVAZ` with QR physical asset tracking, `Eventos CEVAZ`) and commercial apps (`Marago`, `CriptoBro`, `Wallex`, `Yalush`).

## Product Principles

1. **Honest chronology over curated polish:** Show the real progression from `notitas` (first web app) and customer service roles to enterprise MVC and modular API architectures.
2. **Editorial typography over decorative chrome:** Rely on typographic hierarchy, whitespace, hairlines, and tabular dates rather than badges, icons, or cards-in-cards.
3. **Zero dead links or leaked private repos:** Private work shows its architecture and live deployment if public, never a broken or private GitHub URL.
4. **Effortless content editing:** All career items, featured projects, and early archive items live in a single clean data module (`src/data/portfolioData.js`) so the user can update texts in seconds.
